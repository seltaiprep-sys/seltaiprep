💡 How it works

  1. Create Partner — Institute account with 0 credits
  2. Adjust Credits — Add credits manually (+10) or wait for purchase verification
  3. Institute logs in at /ukvi/b2b/login — Sees dashboard, adds students
  4. Institute adds student — Selects date + time, generates unique test link (1 credit deducted)
  5. Student clicks link — Registers username/password, sees scheduled time
  6. Test opens — At scheduled time, student takes UKVI test (24-hour window)
  7. Results — Institute sees all completed tests with scores
