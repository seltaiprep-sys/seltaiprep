💡 How it works

  • Each variant stores all 4 phases — Listening (with audio), Reading, Writing (with chart), and Speaking (all prompts).
  • Audio and chart images are copied to static/full_test_bank/variant_XXX/ on disk.
  • When a user starts a full test, the system picks a variant they haven't taken yet and serves it instantly — no AI cost, no delay.
  • Once served, the variant is locked to that user's attempt (their answers go to a separate session row).
  • Deleting a variant removes both the DB row and the on-disk files.
